King Nebuchadnezzar set up an image of gold on the plain of Dura and commanded every people to bow down to it. Three young men would not. But the God of Israel never dwelt in a statue; He chose to put His Name in a house in Jerusalem.

  1. Digit 1 In 1 Kings, where Solomon builds the temple, find the length in cubits of the inner sanctuary. Divide it by the first digit of your Stage 1 code.
    HintThe building work comes early in Solomon's reign, before the temple is dedicated. Look for the Most Holy Place.
  2. Digit 2 How many articles of clothing are listed on the three men when they were bound and thrown into the furnace?
    HintThe furnace is in the chapter of the golden image.
  3. Digit 3 When the officials gathered round the men who came out of the fire, how many hairs of their heads had been singed?
  4. Digit 4 How many men fell, bound, into the blazing furnace? Count carefully: the king saw more than that walking in it.
