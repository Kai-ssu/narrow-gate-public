{# Discussion questions from section 7 of the Facilitator Guide. #}

Question 1 · The Silent Sovereign

Unseen Providence

God's name is never mentioned in the book of Esther. Yet where did you see His hand orchestrating the timing of events in this game?

Question 2 · Esther 4:14

For Such a Time as This

Mordecai told Esther that if she remained silent, help would come from another place, but she would miss her purpose. What places, schools, or groups has God put you in where you have a chance to stand up for what is right?

Question 3 · Esther 4:16

Courage Over Comfort

In Stage 2, the secret word was FAITH. Esther said, “If I perish, I perish.” Why does real biblical faith require courage even when the outcome isn't guaranteed?
