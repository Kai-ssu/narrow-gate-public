{# Discussion questions from section 6 of the Facilitator Guide. #}

Question 1 · Acts 16:25

Praise in the Dark

Paul and Silas were severely beaten, unjustly accused, and locked in an uncomfortable dungeon. Yet at midnight, they were praying and singing hymns. How do you normally respond when faced with sudden pain, unfairness, or hardship?

Question 2 · Acts 16:27–28

Mercy Over Self-Preservation

When the earthquake hit, the doors opened and the chains fell off. Legally and physically, Paul and Silas could have run away. Instead, they stayed to save the jailer's life. When have you been tempted to take the “easy way out” at the expense of someone else?

Question 3 · Acts 16:30–34

The Domino Effect of Faith

The jailer saw how Paul and Silas reacted during the crisis, which led directly to his question: “What must I do to be saved?” How might your demeanor during tough times open doors to share the Gospel with people around you?
