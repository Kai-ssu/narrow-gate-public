Queen Esther stands between life and death. A message intercepted at the King's Gate is written in an ancient letter-shift cipher.

To reveal the virtue Esther needs to approach the throne, find the Shift Key (K) in Scripture, then decode the scroll:

  1. Step 1 · Find the key Go, gather together all the Jews who are in Susa, and fast for me. Do not eat or drink for ______ days, night or day. Go, gather all the Jews who are present in Shushan, and fast for me; neither eat nor drink for ______ days, night or day. The missing number is your Shift Key (K).
    HintThis is Esther's answer to Mordecai, after he tells her she may have come to her royal position “for such a time as this”.
  2. Step 2 · Decrypt the scroll Shift each letter backward in the alphabet by K places. (If K were 1: B → A, C → B.)
