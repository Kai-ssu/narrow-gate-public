You have been dragged before the magistrates in the Roman agora! Stripped and beaten with rods, your feet are fastened in heavy stocks.

To crack the magistrate's lock, search Acts 16:16–24:

  1. Digit 1 How many apostles were seized and dragged into the marketplace?
    HintRead Acts 16:19–20.

    HintLook at Acts 16:18.
  3. Digit 3 Find the verse where their feet were fastened in the stocks. What is the last digit of that verse number?
    HintIt's the last verse of the passage you're searching.
