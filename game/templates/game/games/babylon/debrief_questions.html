Question 1 · Without a map

Knowing the Whole Story

Question 2 · Daniel 3:17–18

Steadfast in the Fire

The three men stood firm without knowing whether God would save them: “even if he does not” (NIV), “but if not” (NKJV). What does that kind of faith look like for you?

Question 3 · Daniel 4:37; 5:22–23

The Poison of Pride

The secret word was PRIDE. Nebuchadnezzar was humbled and praised God; Belshazzar knew all this and still lifted himself up, and fell that very night. Why is humility the only posture that lasts?
