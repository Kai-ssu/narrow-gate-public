Haman is dead, but his decree still stands: no edict sealed with the king's signet ring can be revoked. A new decree of deliverance must be written!

Search the Scriptures to find the four digits:

  1. Digit 1 After Haman's fall, the king hands over what had been Haman's. How many people receive something of his?
    HintIt happens on the very day Haman dies.
  2. Digit 2 The royal secretariesking's scribes were summoned in the month of Sivan to write the new decree. Which month of the year is Sivan?
    HintThe verse that names Sivan also gives its number.
  3. Digit 3 When Mordecai left the king's presence, how many crowns of gold did he wear?
    HintRead how Mordecai is dressed as he leaves the king, once the new decree has been sent out.
  4. Digit 4 On how many days of the month of Adar do the Jews hold their yearly feast of joy and sending gifts?
    HintLook near the end of the book, where Mordecai writes to make the feast a yearly celebration. The days are named.
