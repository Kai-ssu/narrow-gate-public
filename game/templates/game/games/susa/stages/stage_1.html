Haman's decree has gone out to every province. To uncover the roots of his plot and open the second archive, you must search the royal records.

Search the Scriptures to find the four digits:

  1. Digit 1 Where Mordecai is first introduced, how many of his ancestors are named?
    HintMordecai is introduced with his family line and his tribe, just before we meet Esther. Count each name that follows “son of”.
  2. Digit 2 In the twelfth year of King XerxesKing Ahasuerus, in which month of the year did they begin casting the purPur (the lot) before Haman?
    HintThe lot is cast soon after Haman is promoted and Mordecai refuses to bow. The verse names the month of Nisan: which month of the year is it?
  3. Digit 3 How many of the king's officers who guarded the doorway conspired to assassinate King Xerxes before Mordecai exposed them? How many of the king's eunuchs, the doorkeepers, sought to lay hands on King Ahasuerus before Mordecai exposed them?
    HintMordecai uncovers the plot while sitting at the king's gate, not long after Esther becomes queen. The plotters are named.
  4. Digit 4 Haman's letters were sent to all the king's provinces. How many verbs of destruction do they command against the Jews?
    HintRight after the king's scribes write out Haman's orders, couriers carry the letters to every province. Count the actions the order commands.
