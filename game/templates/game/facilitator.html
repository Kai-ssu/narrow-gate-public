Running the Game

Everything for the night, in order. Allow about {{ game.minutes }} minutes for the game, plus 10–15 minutes for the debrief, with teams of {{ game.team_size }}.

1 · Before players arrive

  1. On the projector laptop, open the backdrop and press F for full screen.
  2. Players scan the QR code on the backdrop, or type the address below. One phone per team works best.
  3. Make sure every team has an NIV or NKJV Bible. Teams choose which one in the app before they begin.
Open the backdrop Backdrop without timer

2 · Start

  1. Play the story on the projector: choose Story on the backdrop (or press I). It tells the backstory in about two minutes and ends with Start the game, which starts the room timer. It's a dramatized retelling, so encourage everyone to read the full passage in their Bible afterwards. You can also read the host script aloud instead.
  2. When the timer starts (or you press Space on the backdrop), tell every team to tap Begin at the same moment.

3 · During the game

    {% include game.templates.lead_tips %}
  1. Each clue has its own hint button, so step in only when a team has been stuck for a while.
  2. When a team escapes, open their Victory Box. Reset for a new team on their screen clears the phone for the next group.

4 · Debrief

  1. On the backdrop, move the mouse and choose Debrief (or press D).
  2. Use → and ←, or a presentation clicker, to move through the three questions.
