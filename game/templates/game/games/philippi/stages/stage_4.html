The trembling jailer falls before you with a torch in hand and asks: Sirs, what must I do to be saved?

Read Acts 16:30–34 to calculate the master freedom code:

  1. Digit 1 Count the letters in the very first command given to the jailer in Acts 16:31.
    HintIt's the first word of Paul and Silas's answer to the jailer's question.
  2. Digit 2 The last digit of the verse where, at that hour of the night, the jailer washed their wounds. The last digit of the verse where, that same hour of the night, the jailer washed their stripes.
  3. Digit 3 How many prisoners abandoned the jailer to face Roman execution?
    HintThink back to what Paul shouted when the jailer drew his sword.
  4. Digit 4 In verse 34, how many changes of heart are named in the jailer and his household? Count what they felt and what they came to do toward God, not what he did for Paul and Silas.
    HintLeave out the meal.
