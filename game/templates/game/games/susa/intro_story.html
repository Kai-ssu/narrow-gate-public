The year is 474 BC. You stand within the grand winter citadel of Susa, imperial capital of the Persian Empire.

Outside these palace walls, darkness looms over the Jewish nation. Haman the Agagite, prime minister to King XerxesKing Ahasuerus, has cast the Pur (the lot) and secured an irrevocable royal decree ordering the slaughter of every Jewish man, woman, and child on a single day.

You are trusted scribes and confidants within the household of Mordecai and Queen Esther. Mordecai has sent word through the King's Gate, but royal guards and informers surround the court.

To save our people, Queen Esther must enter the king's inner court unsummoned: an act punishable by death, unless the king holds out his golden scepter. But Esther needs the strength, wisdom, and undeniable facts to unmask Haman before the king.

Four locked archives sit before you: ancestral records, intercepted scrolls, royal court chronicles, and official decrees. Seek the truth in the Scriptures, hold fast to faith, and remember: perhaps you have come to your position for such a time as this.
